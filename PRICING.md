# Pricing

All tiers are **benchmarked against official list price** and land in the
**~0.03–0.3 of official list** range depending on tier and model family.

- **No monthly fee.** Pay as you go.
- **Settlement:** USDT (TRC-20) by default. Other cryptos considered for
  large / wholesale accounts.
- Trial credits are issued on signup — validate before any spend.

## Tiers (indicative)

| Tier | Typical discount vs official list | Who it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ~0.3 | General usage, image, embeddings |
| Pro | ~0.15 | Heavy Claude / coding workloads |
| Wholesale | ~0.09 | High-volume operators, white-label upstream |

Exact per-model rates are returned by the gateway's pricing endpoint and shown
in the dashboard. Rates move with upstream cost; the table above is the
operating band, not a fixed quote.

## Notes

- Discounts reflect aggregated supply economics, not a promotional stunt.
- No lock-in, no minimum commit on standard tiers.
- Region availability is per the gateway's terms of service.
